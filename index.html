The big picture

Why is hiring more than a simple HR question?

Hiring does more than shape careers. It affects how companies operate, the opportunities open to individuals, society and the economy as a whole.

How organisations look for and select people determines which skills and abilities they recognise and value, who gains access to professional opportunities, and how effectively human potential is put to use.

Yet hiring practices have not kept pace with the transformation of the world of work. Technological progress, AI-driven processes, economic uncertainty and rapidly changing skill demands are reshaping the labour market from the ground up. Meanwhile, recruitment and selection in many cases still rest on previous job titles, rigid requirements and opaque screening processes.

The consequences reach far beyond a poor candidate experience:

  • Valuable human skills and opportunities go unrecognised and unused.
  • The lack of transparency and accountability undermines trust in hiring.
  • Inadequate recruitment and selection practices can deepen inequalities of opportunity and impair the efficient functioning of the labour market.

Humanize Hiring believes that renewing hiring is not just an HR matter but a social and economic challenge that calls for shared attention and action.

Our mission

Renewing hiring through the joint effort of everyone who shapes and experiences how it works. We turn real experience and evidence-based knowledge into practical standards, better labour-market practices and lasting change.

Our vision

We want a labour market where people are recognised and valued for their real abilities and potential to grow; where fair and transparent hiring practices make professional opportunities more accessible to everyone; and where recruitment and selection create long-term value for individuals, organisations and society.

Why now

Why now?

Four defining challenges make change increasingly urgent.

1

Technological transformation

Artificial intelligence and automation are changing how organisations find, screen and assess potential colleagues. These technologies make more efficient and better-founded hiring possible, but they also raise important questions about fairness, transparency, human oversight and accountability.

2

Skills shortages and hidden talent

While organisations increasingly report difficulty finding the right professionals, rigid job requirements and templated pre-screening methods often prevent relevant abilities and transferable skills from being recognised.

3

Eroding trust in hiring

Vague job ads, opaque assessment criteria, excessive or job-unrelated test assignments, incomplete communication and rejections without explanation breed frustration and undermine trust on both sides of the hiring process.

4

Untapped human potential

People’s real abilities, experience and potential to grow cannot always be recognised from job titles or linear career paths. Recognising these abilities better can open new opportunities for individuals and create significant value for organisations and the economy.

Principles

Our four core pillars

Humanize Hiring’s work rests on four mutually reinforcing principles.

  1. 01

    Recognising skills and potential

    Recognising people’s real abilities, transferable skills and potential to grow, beyond previous job titles and conventional career paths.

    Encouraging skills-based approaches that help organisations identify relevant talent and widen access to professional opportunities.

  2. 02

    Meaningful transparency

    Making hiring processes understandable, predictable and transparent.

    Promoting clear job ads, realistic expectations, unambiguous assessment criteria, honest communication and meaningful feedback.

  3. 03

    Fairness, dignity and accountability

    Promoting that candidates are treated with dignity and respect throughout the entire hiring process.

    Supporting fair and consistent assessment, proportionate selection methods, timely information, and clear responsibility for decisions and the practices used.

  4. 04

    Responsible technology and AI

    Encouraging the responsible use of technology and artificial intelligence in recruitment and selection.

    Strengthening appropriate human oversight, transparency and accountability, and promoting safeguards against unfair or unjustified decisions.

    Technology should support better decisions, not replace human responsibility.

Process

The entire hiring process

Human-centred hiring cannot be achieved by improving a single sub-process.

Humanize Hiring’s framework looks at the whole process, from first impression to final decision.

  1. Job posting

    • Accurate descriptions that reflect the real role and tasks.
    • Clear responsibilities and expectations.
    • Transparent pay and benefits information.
    • Requirements aligned with the actual needs of the job.

  2. Sourcing and initial assessment

    • Recognising relevant skills and transferable abilities.
    • Extending assessment beyond keywords and previous job titles.
    • Proportionate and responsible use of automated pre-screening and artificial intelligence.
    • Human oversight and fair assessment practices.

  3. Selection and assessment

    • Clear and consistent assessment criteria.
    • Relevant and proportionate interview processes.
    • Reasonable, job-related test assignments.
    • Respect for candidates’ time and invested effort.

  4. Communication and decision-making

    • Predictable timelines and meaningful updates.
    • Clear communication of decisions and outcomes.
    • Constructive feedback where justified and feasible.
    • Taking responsibility throughout the process.

Our aim is not to impose a single, uniform hiring process on every organisation, but to define consistent principles and measurable expectations that can be applied across different industries, roles and labour markets.

In development

The Standard

People-First Hiring Certified

An independent international standard for fair and transparent hiring

One of Humanize Hiring’s central long-term goals is to create an independent, internationally applicable employer certification system.

The People-First Hiring Certified certification recognises organisations that demonstrate their commitment to human-centred hiring through measurable practices and evidence subject to independent assessment.

The certification system translates the four core pillars into practical assessment criteria and verifiable organisational requirements.

What does the certification offer?

  • For candidates:

    a credible reference point for judging employers’ hiring practices.

  • For employers:

    a practical framework for assessing and improving their recruitment and selection processes.

  • For the labour market as a whole:

    a reliable benchmark for fair, transparent and accountable hiring practices.

The certification framework is being developed gradually, building on research, stakeholder involvement and pilot programmes.

Independence Under incorporation

Independence & Governance

Humanize Hiring Association is an independent, non-profit professional association currently under incorporation in Hungary, with an international mission and scope.

Credible standards require clear governance. To ensure that our research, benchmarks and future certification remain objective and trusted across the labour market, our work is guided by four core governance principles:

  • Open, multi-stakeholder collaboration:We welcome founders, employers, HR practitioners, researchers, candidates and solution providers to contribute expertise, insight and pilot experience to advance human-centred hiring.
  • Merit-based, transparent criteria:The People-First Hiring Certified standard is built on documented, publicly accessible criteria—evaluated consistently against verifiable evidence rather than status or affiliation.
  • Separation of standard-setting and assessment:Certification evaluations are conducted independently of commercial partnerships, sponsorships or community participation, ensuring equal conditions for every applicant organisation.
  • Transparent conflict-of-interest management:Any potential overlap between commercial activities, advisory roles and governance responsibilities is openly disclosed and managed through clear recusal rules.

The certification cannot rest on a signed statement or public pledge alone.

Its credibility comes from transparent criteria, a documented assessment methodology, independent adjudication, regular review, and safeguards for managing conflicts of interest.

International

International aim, Hungarian foundation

Humanize Hiring is being established in Hungary, and its initial activities and pilot programmes focus on Hungary and the Central and Eastern European region.

Its identity and aim, however, have been international from the start.

The initiative aims to develop principles, research and professional standards that can be applied across different labour markets, taking into account differences in local employment practices, legal frameworks and cultural characteristics.

To this end, we build international partnerships and work with professional communities, employers, researchers and organisations committed to improving hiring practices.
